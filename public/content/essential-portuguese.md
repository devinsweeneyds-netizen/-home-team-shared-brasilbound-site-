# Essential Portuguese for Travelers

While you can get by with English in high-end hotels and major tourist spots in Rio or São Paulo, most Brazilians do not speak fluent English. Knowing just a few phrases will not only help you navigate but will also earn you massive respect from the locals.

**Note on Pronunciation**: Portuguese is a phonetic language, but it has some sounds that are tricky for English speakers. 
- **"R"** at the beginning of a word or double "RR" sounds like a soft English "H" (e.g., *Rio* is pronounced "Hee-oh").
- **"LH"** sounds like the "li" in "million."
- **"NH"** sounds like the "ny" in "canyon."
- **"ÃO"** is a nasal sound, like saying "ow" while pinching your nose.

### The Essentials
- **Hello / Hi**: *Olá / Oi* (Oh-LAH / Oy)
- **Good morning**: *Bom dia* (Boh-jee-ah)
- **Good afternoon**: *Boa tarde* (Boh-ahr-jee)
- **Good night**: *Boa noite* (Boh-noy-chee)
- **Please**: *Por favor* (Poor-fah-vohr)
- **Thank you (Male)**: *Obrigado* (Oh-bree-GAH-doo)
- **Thank you (Female)**: *Obrigada* (Oh-bree-GAH-dah)
- **You're welcome**: *De nada* (Jee-nah-dah)
- **Excuse me / Sorry**: *Com licença / Desculpe* (Koh-lee-SAYN-sah / Jes-KOOL-pee)

### Getting Around
- **Where is...?**: *Onde fica...?* (Own-jee-fee-kah)
- **The bathroom**: *O banheiro* (Oh-bahn-YAY-roo)
- **The beach**: *A praia* (Ah-PRY-ah)
- **The hotel**: *O hotel* (Oh-oh-TELL)
- **A taxi / An Uber**: *Um táxi / Um Uber*
- **Right / Left**: *Direita / Esquerda* (Jee-RAY-tah / Es-KEHR-dah)
- **Straight ahead**: *Direto* (Jee-REH-too)

### Dining & Shopping
- **The bill, please**: *A conta, por favor* (Ah-KOHN-tah, poor-fah-vohr)
- **How much is it?**: *Quanto custa?* (KWAHN-too-KOOS-tah)
- **I would like...**: *Eu queria...* (Eh-oo-kay-REE-ah)
- **Water**: *Água* (Ah-gwah)
- **Beer**: *Cerveja* (Sehr-VAY-zhah)
- **Delicious**: *Delicioso / Muito bom* (Day-lee-see-OH-zoo / MOOY-too-bohm)
- **Do you accept cards?**: *Aceita cartão?* (Ah-SAY-tah-kahr-TOWN)

### Survival & Safety
- **I don't speak Portuguese**: *Não falo português* (Nown-FAH-loo-poor-too-GAYS)
- **Do you speak English?**: *Você fala inglês?* (Voh-SAY-FAH-lah-een-GLAYS)
- **Help!**: *Socorro!* (Soh-KOH-hoo)
- **Police**: *Polícia* (Poh-LEE-syah)

### The "Brazilian Slang" Starter Pack
Using these will make you sound like a local:
- **Tudo bem?**: This is the universal "How's it going? / Everything good?". You can answer with the same phrase: "Tudo bem!"
- **Legal**: (Lay-GAHL) Means "Cool" or "Nice."
- **Valeu**: (Vah-LAY-oo) A very informal "Thanks" or "Bye."
- **Cara**: (KAH-rah) Informal for "Dude" or "Guy."

### Pro Tip for Americans
The most important word you can learn is **"Obrigado"** (or **"Obrigada"**). Use it constantly. Brazilians are very polite in their social interactions, and a little Portuguese goes a long way in turning a standard transaction into a friendly exchange. If you are struggling, just smile and use Google Translate—most Brazilians are patient and happy to help you figure it out.
