Brazil Survival Kit

Navigate Brazil with Confidence

The essential one-page checklist for American travelers.

🛂 1. Documents & Entry

  • Passport Validity: 6+ months from entry date.
  • Blank Pages: 1-2 empty "Visa" pages.
  • Visa Status: Exemption until April 10, 2025. After that, carry your printed e-Visa ($80.90).
  • Onward Travel: Printed flight confirmation.
  • Insurance: Policy with Medical Evacuation.

🛡️ 2. Health & Safety

  • The "Golden Rule": Não Dê Bobeira. Keep your phone hidden; step into a shop to use it.
  • Street Smart: No expensive jewelry or watches.
  • Water: Bottled/Filtered only. No tap water.
  • Repellent: 25% DEET for Dengue/Zika.
  • Vaccines: Yellow Fever (10 days prior).
  • Emergency Numbers:
    190: Police
    192: Ambulance
    193: Fire
    US Embassy (BSB): +55 (61) 3312-7000
    US Consulate (Rio): +55 (21) 3823-2000

💰 3. Money & Payments

  • Notify Bank: Set travel notice for Brazil.
  • Cards: Visa/MC with Tap-to-Pay.
  • The PIX Gap: Carry R$2, 5, 10 bills for change.
  • Secure ATMs: Inside bank branches only.

📱 4. Communication & Tech

  • WhatsApp: Verify before you leave US.
  • eSIM: Buy data plan (Airalo/Holafly) early.
  • Power: Type N adapter (3 round pins).
  • VPN: Essential for public/hotel WiFi.
  • Key Phrases:
    Tudo bem? (How's it going?)
    Obrigado/a (Thank you)
    A conta, por favor (The bill)
    Aceita cartão? (Accept card?)

📦 5. Packing Must-Haves

  • Sunscreen: Pack double (expensive in BR).
  • Light Fabrics: Linen/Cotton only.
  • Rain Gear: Compact umbrella/poncho.
  • Power Bank: For high GPS usage.

💡 Pro Tip: The "Jeitinho Brasileiro"

If you hit a roadblock, a smile and a polite "Could we find a way?" goes far. Brazilians value personal connection and creativity in problem-solving.
