# 🇧🇷 Brazil Survival Kit: The Ultimate Traveler's Checklist

*A concise, printable guide for American travelers. Save this to your phone or print it out before you head to the airport.*

---

## 🛂 1. Documents & Entry
- [ ] **Passport Validity**: Ensure at least 6 months remain from your date of entry.
- [ ] **Blank Pages**: At least 1-2 completely blank visa pages.
- [ ] **Visa Status**: 
    - *Before April 10, 2025*: No visa required for US citizens.
    - *After April 10, 2025*: Ensure you have your printed **e-Visa**.
- [ ] **Proof of Onward Travel**: Have a copy of your return flight confirmation.
- [ ] **Travel Insurance**: Printed policy with emergency contact number.

## 🛡️ 2. Safety & Health
- [ ] **"Não Dê Bobeira"**: Never walk with your phone in your hand. Step inside a shop to check maps.
- [ ] **Emergency Numbers**: 
    - **190**: Police
    - **192**: Ambulance (SAMU)
- [ ] **Repellent**: High-DEET (25%+) or Picaridin to prevent Dengue/Zika.
- [ ] **Water**: Drink **bottled or filtered water only**. Avoid tap water.
- [ ] **Vaccines**: Confirm you have your Yellow Fever shot (highly recommended).

## 💰 3. Money & Payments
- [ ] **Notify Bank**: Set travel notices on all credit/debit cards.
- [ ] **Cards**: Bring at least one Visa and one Mastercard (No-foreign-transaction-fee preferred).
- [ ] **Small Bills**: Carry small Reais (R$2, 5, 10) for "troco" (change) at small vendors.
- [ ] **Wise/Nomad**: Highly recommended for the best exchange rates.

## 📱 4. Tech & Connectivity
- [ ] **WhatsApp**: Install and verify before leaving the US. This is the primary way to contact hotels, guides, and restaurants.
- [ ] **eSIM**: Pre-purchase a data plan via Airalo or Holafly.
- [ ] **Power Adapter**: Buy a **Type N** (three round pins) adapter.
- [ ] **Offline Maps**: Download your destination city on Google Maps.

## 🚕 5. Getting Around
- [ ] **Apps**: Download **Uber** and **99** (the local competitor).
- [ ] **Domestic Flights**: Use Azul, LATAM, or GOL for intercity travel.
- [ ] **Metro**: Safe and efficient in Rio and São Paulo during daylight hours.

## 🗣️ 6. Quick Phrases
- [ ] **Tudo bem?**: How's it going? / Everything good?
- [ ] **Obrigado (m) / Obrigada (f)**: Thank you.
- [ ] **A conta, por favor**: The bill, please.
- [ ] **Quanto custa?**: How much is it?
- [ ] **Onde fica...?**: Where is...?

## 🍴 7. Food & Etiquette
- [ ] **Dining**: Use a napkin to hold pizza, burgers, and coxinhas.
- [ ] **Tipping**: 10% is usually included. No need to add more unless service was stellar.
- [ ] **"Kilo" Restaurants**: The best way to try a variety of local food quickly and cheaply.

## 📦 8. Packing Essentials
- [ ] **Sunscreen**: Pack plenty from home; it's very expensive in Brazil.
- [ ] **Light Fabrics**: Cotton and linen for the humidity.
- [ ] **Havaianas**: Buy your flip-flops in Brazil—they’re a national treasure and much cheaper!
